Recover MongoDB database

On my PC
C:\Users\1070207\AppData\Local\mongodb\mongodb-database-tools-windows\bin\mongodump.exe

On the VM in PowerShell
New-Item -ItemType Directory -Path "C:\MongoBackup" -Force

& "C:\Program Files\MongoDB\Server\8.0\bin\mongodump.exe" `
  --uri="mongodb://localhost:27017/mics_filenet_sync" `
  --archive="C:\MongoBackup\mics_filenet.gz" `
  --gzip

Copy the file created on the VM to our PC, into:
C:\Users\1070207\AppData\Local\mongodb\mongodb-database-tools-windows\bin

& "C:\Users\1070207\AppData\Local\mongodb\mongodb-database-tools-windows\bin\mongorestore.exe" `
  --uri="mongodb://localhost:27017" `
  --archive="C:\Users\1070207\AppData\Local\mongodb\mongodb-database-tools-windows\bin\mics_filenet.gz" ` -- rename the file
  --gzip `
  --drop
